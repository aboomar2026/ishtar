Netlify's [Functions overview](https://docs.netlify.com/build/functions/overview/) additionally informs serverless architecture: choose a bounded poll initially instead of presuming a persistent process/socket.

## Proposed visual direction

Warm premium editorial presentation using approved burgundy/cream/copper, the existing Tajawal/El Messiri pairing, clear product photography and strong but legible actions. Keep the three current hero profiles with independent admin content; distinguish campaign-slide timing from 60-second layout rotation in labels. Decorative accessories remain noninteractive/hidden from assistive technology; product imagery remains clearly clickable. Never represent decorative stars as real ratings, carts as fake sales, or a truck as a promise of free delivery.

Preserve product-rich merchandising and existing overlapping grids per the owner's explicit instruction. Add lightweight recent views/favorites/curated looks where they help decisions; do not remove products merely to imitate a sparse competitor. No Three.js at this stage: no approved product 3D assets or measured mobile performance case exists.
